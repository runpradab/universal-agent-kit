# Wiki Schema — Company Workspace
> Page format and rules: UNIVERSAL-KIT.md Part 4.3.
> One page per entity. Agents answer from the wiki, not from memory.

## Categories

- `finance/` — chart of accounts, recurring transactions, banks,
  fiscal calendar. Owned mainly by the accountant agent.
- `policies/` — expense policy, approval thresholds, any "how we do
  things" rule. The accountant cites these pages per decision.
- `vendors/` — one page per significant vendor/supplier: what we buy,
  typical amounts, payment terms, contacts.
- `markets/` — one page per market/segment we operate in or study.
  Owned mainly by the market-researcher agent.
- `competitors/` — one page per competitor: offering, pricing,
  positioning, sources + dates.
- `customers/` — segments and key accounts (no sensitive personal
  data beyond what the task needs).
- `processes/` — repeatable procedures: month-end close checklist,
  research report format, onboarding steps.

## Extra rule for this workspace
External facts (markets/, competitors/) must carry source + date on
every claim — research data goes stale; undated facts are worthless.
